## Suggested LinkedIn caption

I've been building Mom's Bakery: a small web application for my mother's homemade cake business in Ukraine.

The design constraint was personal: it should be easy for someone in her 60s to use after one short explanation.

Customers can browse her real cakes, reserve available desserts without creating an account, or request a custom cake. Her private dashboard brings together orders, stock, production costs and estimated profit.

Under the hood: Python, SQLite and vanilla JavaScript. The practical work includes preventing overselling, handling duplicate submissions safely, keeping customer information private, and preserving historical costs when product prices change.

I developed the MVP with Codex, iterating on the workflows and testing on iPhone. It's still a local prototype; production deployment and real-device push notification testing are next. The screenshots use fictional orders and finances.

Repository: https://github.com/Timuridzone/moms-bakery

## Short project description

A Ukrainian bakery MVP with account-free reservations, custom cake requests, a private owner dashboard, transactional stock management and cost-based profit estimates. Built with Python, SQLite and vanilla JavaScript; designed around a real family business.
